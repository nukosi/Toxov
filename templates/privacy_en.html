Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 11, 2026

Toxov ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Toxov service. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Username and password (stored as a hashed value)
  • Email address (optional, used for password reset)
  • Usage data (block settings, streak count, points, etc.)
  • Payment information collected by Stripe (credit card details are never stored on our servers)
  • Access logs (IP address, timestamps, etc.)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the service
  • To manage subscriptions and process payments
  • To provide user support (e.g. password reset)
  • To improve the service and perform anonymized analytics
  • To investigate and respond to violations of our terms

3. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following cases:

  • When required by law
  • With Stripe, Inc. for payment processing (Stripe's privacy policy applies)
  • With Resend, Inc. for email delivery

4. Security

  • All communications are encrypted via HTTPS
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt
  • Credit card data is managed by Stripe and never stored on our servers
  • Login attempts are rate-limited to prevent unauthorized access

5. Access, Correction, and Deletion

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, please contact us at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Cookies

We use session cookies solely to maintain your logged-in state.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. We will notify you of any significant changes through the service.

8. Contact
